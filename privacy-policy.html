ER Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Application: ER Assistant Developer type: Individual Developer Contact: erassistantsupport@gmail.com

1. Introduction

ER Assistant is an educational emergency medicine reference and clinical calculator application.

This policy explains how the application handles information stored on your device and information you choose to share.

2. Information Stored Locally

The application uses local device storage to maintain:

  • Favorite clinical calculators.
  • Favorite medications and emergency protocols.
  • Recently viewed emergency protocols.
  • Medication reference verification statuses and dates.
  • Reviewer names and notes entered by users.
  • Medication verification history.

The reviewed application code stores these records locally. The application does not require users to create an account.

3. Information Entered by Users

Medication verification features may allow users to enter reviewer names and free-text notes.

Do not enter patient names, medical record numbers, or other identifiable patient information into these fields.

4. Exporting and Sharing Information

Users may export medication verification reports and verification history as CSV files.

These exports may include reviewer names, dates, verification statuses, and notes.

Exporting or sharing is initiated by the user. Once information is shared with another application or service, that recipient's privacy practices may apply.

5. External Medical References

ER Assistant may open medical reference websites through external applications or browsers.

External websites operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Storage and Retention

Application preferences and verification records may remain stored locally until changed or deleted.

Uninstalling the application generally removes its local data, subject to Android backup and restoration settings.

Temporary exported files may remain on the device for a period determined by the operating system.

7. Security

ER Assistant relies on Android application storage protections.

Users should protect their devices against unauthorized access. Information exported or shared outside the application may be subject to different security protections.

8. Medical Disclaimer

ER Assistant provides educational medical reference information.

It does not replace professional clinical judgment, institutional protocols, or independent verification of treatment recommendations and medication doses.

9. Children's Privacy

ER Assistant is intended for healthcare professionals and adult learners and is not designed for children.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the application develops. The published policy will indicate the latest revision date.
